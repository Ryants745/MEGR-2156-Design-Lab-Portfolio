Task B: Product Analysis

Dumbbell weight

A. Primary Engineering Function

The primary engineering function of a dumbbell is to provide a certain amount of force and weight centered across the handle, creating a static loading condition and arm resistance to oppose the muscles when joints rotate when lifting them. 

B. Governing Model

i. Model and Variables

The kinetic torque resistance exerted at a human pivot joint by a dumbbell going through angular motion is governed by Rigid Body Rotational Dynamics

τ= r*m(g + ay)sin(θ) + I*α

m= mass of the dumbbell

g= acceleration of gravity

ay= linear vertical acceleration

r= arm distance from the joint to the center of mass of the dumbbell

θ= offset of the lever arm relative to the vertical line of gravitational action

I= moment of inertia of the dumbbell 

α= angular acceleration of the dumbbell

ii. Governing Assumption

Mass Distribution or Center of Mass: the the material density is symmetric so the center of gravity sits exactly in the center of the handle.

Solid Iron Body

The hexagonal shape geometry on both ends provide flat faces that eliminate rolling across inclined surfaces translating motion by high friction when it is at rest. The handle is wide near the center of the dumbbell that causes even distribution of weight through our hands to reduce pressure. The diamond rigid ends engraved into the handle creates a mechanical lock with our hands causing the static friction to increase when being used. 

D. Patent Identification

The patent number of the hexagonal dumbbell assembly is US6099443A and the data is provided by the IFI CLAIMS Patent Services.

i. Alternate Solutions: 

An alternative solution is an adjustable dumbbell that uses internal latches and locks a certain amount of weight and plates onto a central handle. another solution would be hydro dumbbells which use hollow plastic that is filled with water to generate weight when being used.

ii. Key Design Decision

The engineer selected a hexagonal cross section for the weight heads instead of a spheres or cylinders. I think they made this choice so that the weight does not roll on uneven surfaces to provide more safety to those who use it and those who might be around it. The hexagon geometry provides flat surfaces that keep the dumbbell at rest while also allowing it to have other uses such as a way to push our body up during workouts.
